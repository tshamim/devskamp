Module 1

The Crypto Battlefield

Cryptocurrency is the most volatile, 24/7 financial market in human history. To survive and thrive, you must first understand the macroeconomic cycles that drive institutional and retail money.

01. Spot vs. Margin

Spot Trading is buying the actual asset (e.g., holding 1 BTC). Margin/Derivatives involves borrowing funds to amplify gains (and losses).

Rule #1: Never touch margin until you are profitable on Spot for 6 straight months.

02. Liquidity

Crypto markets are driven by liquidity. Large players (Whales) need volume to enter/exit positions. They often push prices past obvious support/resistance levels to trigger retail stop-losses, creating the liquidity they need.

03. Narrative

Tech is secondary; narrative is primary. Whether it's L1s, DeFi, NFTs, AI, or Memecoins, capital rotates based on attention. Pro traders track the narrative flow of capital, not just the underlying code.

The Psychology of a Market Cycle

Markets move in distinct phases driven by human emotion: Fear and Greed. Identify where we are in the cycle to bias your trading direction.

1. Accumulation

Smart money buys quietly while retail is bored or fearful.

2. Markup (Bull)

Trend is established. Retail enters. Greed takes over.

3. Distribution

Smart money sells to euphoric retail. High volatility.

4. Markdown (Bear)

Panic selling. Price crashes back to intrinsic value.

Module 2

Technical Analysis (TA)

TA is the study of past price action to predict future probabilities. It is not a crystal ball; it is a framework for defining risk and identifying areas where supply and demand are likely to imbalance.

1. Reading Candlesticks

Candlesticks display price action over a specific timeframe (e.g., 1 Hour, 1 Day). They tell a story of the battle between buyers (bulls) and sellers (bears).

  • ▲
    The Body: Shows the difference between the open and close price. A large body indicates strong momentum.
  • |
    The Wick (Shadow): Shows the highest and lowest prices reached during the timeframe. Long wicks indicate rejection.

2. Trend Analysis & Moving Averages

"The trend is your friend until the end when it bends." Moving Averages (MA) smooth out price data to identify the overarching trend.

ℹ Pro Tip: Confluence

Never trade based on one indicator. Look for confluence—when multiple signals align (e.g., price hits structural support AND the RSI is oversold AND a bullish candlestick pattern forms).

3. Classic Chart Patterns

Patterns reveal the psychological battle between buyers and sellers. Pros use them to identify breakout zones and set logical stop losses.

The Pro Position Sizer

Amateurs buy a random dollar amount (e.g., "$500 of ETH"). Pros risk a precise percentage of their total equity. Calculate your exact position size below.

Amount at Risk: $100.00
Total Position Size: $2,000.00

Win Rate vs. Reward/Risk (R:R)

You do not need to be right 80% of the time. If your winners are 3x larger than your losers, you only need a 30% win rate to be profitable.

Why Retail Traders Fail
